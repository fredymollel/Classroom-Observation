Competency-based instructional practicesKPI 1.2KPI 3.1

Practices follow the lesson plan stages. For each one, look at what the teacher and the students are doing, then rate the practice. Where useful, attach a photo or video as evidence.

Evident: clearly seen for most of the lesson or most students. Somewhat evident: seen partly, briefly or with only some students. Not evident: there was a chance to see it, but it did not happen. Not observed: no chance to see it in this lesson (not a negative mark).

Overall, how was the lesson taught?KPI 1.2

Teacher-led: students mostly listen, answer questions and copy notes. Competency-based (target): students ask questions, discuss, practise in activities and connect learning to their lives. Student-led: students explain reasoning, assess each other and lead activities. Student-driven: students set their own goals and pursue their own questions.
